## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-05

The first release.

### Added

- 🎄 The Christmas season: falling snow that settles on windows and the Dock, rides along with windows and flies off when they're shaken, plus holiday lights along the Dock and the top of the screen.
- Settings window with live previews, and a menu bar panel with quick toggles.
- Per-app exceptions, hiding for full-screen apps, and display selection.
- One-command install, and release scripts.
