## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-09

The winter fun update.

### Added

- ⛄ **Snow Play.** Hold ⌃⌥ (or turn on Snow Play in the menu bar) and drag a drift to make a snowball. Roll it to make it bigger, flick to throw it, and watch it splat against the edge of the screen. Stack three and a snowman comes to life with a hat, a scarf, a carrot nose and stick arms. Drag a snowman to carry it somewhere else, or double-click it to melt it.
- 🎅 **Santa's sleigh** flies by now and then, trailing sparkles and dropping presents. Open one and a card rises out of it with a fact about the season.
- 🎶 **Lights that follow music.** While Apple Music or Spotify plays, the lights flash with the beat and step through their colours.
- 💡 **More lights.** Ten new palettes (Gold, Peppermint, Frosted, Fireside, Forest, Royal, Nordic, Pastel, Neon and Rainbow) and three new effects (Sparkle, Alternate and Color Wave).
- ✨ **Lights along the Dock's rim.** A new default style follows the Dock's rounded glass with the bulbs standing up, so they never cover an icon. The draped style now hangs a bulb in every gap between icons.
- ❄️ Frost in the corners of the screen, a Let It Snow blizzard, and a countdown to Christmas in the menu bar.
- 🖥️ Choose what each display shows: snow, Snow Play, lights, frost and Santa, display by display.
- 🔄 **Automatic updates.** SeasonMyMac checks for new versions once a day and installs them with one click.

### Changed

- A cleaner menu bar panel, laid out like the system's own, and Settings redesigned to match System Settings.
- The menu bar shows the app's icon.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-05

The first release.

### Added

- 🎄 The Christmas season: falling snow that settles on windows and the Dock, rides along with windows and flies off when they're shaken, plus holiday lights along the Dock and the top of the screen.
- Settings window with live previews, and a menu bar panel with quick toggles.
- Per-app exceptions, hiding for full-screen apps, and display selection.
- One-command install, and release scripts.
